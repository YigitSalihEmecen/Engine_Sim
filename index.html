Live inputs
driven by a game, or by you

          
These are not part of the sound file — they are what is happening to the engine right now. A game
            drives them every frame through sim.setInputs(); here they are sliders. Try a scene, then rev it.

          

          

        


        

          
Sound & mix

          
Balance between the synthesis voices and the fixed-frequency tone. Rumble is the
            firing-pulsed body of the engine; Bass punch adds the harmonics that let small speakers carry the low end.

          

            
Voices
Pops route mostly through the exhaust pipe, so Pops and Exhaust interact.

            
Tone
Compression is a three-band stage that only ever pulls down.

Engine tuning

          
Every value here is part of the preset. Geometry changes — pipe lengths, pulse shape, plenum —
            rebuild the wavetables and waveguides, so they settle a moment after you let go.
